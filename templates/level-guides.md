# Level Guides

## Beginner

### Main Goal

Help the learner survive everyday situations with short, usable Korean.

### Best Lesson Features

- Short dialogues
- High-frequency expressions
- Repetition of one sentence pattern
- Simple pronunciation focus
- Clear honorific or politeness guidance

### Keep in Mind

- Avoid too much grammar in one lesson.
- Use many examples and slow repetition.
- Teach set phrases first, then small variations.
- Include native-language support when helpful.

### Good Topics

- Greeting
- Introducing yourself
- Cafe ordering
- Shopping
- Asking for directions
- Booking an appointment

## Intermediate

### Main Goal

Help the learner sound more natural and flexible in daily conversation.

### Best Lesson Features

- Longer dialogues
- Similar expression comparison
- Connected sentences
- More spontaneous roleplay
- Error correction with explanation

### Keep in Mind

- Compare nuance between similar forms.
- Expand from sentence to short conversation.
- Add reasons, preferences, and experiences.
- Train both polite and casual speech when relevant.

### Good Topics

- Making plans
- Talking about past experiences
- Giving opinions
- Solving small problems
- Workplace communication
- Social conversations

## Advanced

### Main Goal

Help the learner express nuance, social awareness, and natural style.

### Best Lesson Features

- Authentic conversation flow
- Register and tone control
- Nuance-focused corrections
- Formal and informal contrast
- Open-ended speaking tasks

### Keep in Mind

- Prioritize naturalness over literal accuracy.
- Teach hidden social meaning and softening devices.
- Show what sounds awkward even if grammatically correct.
- Use discussion and reformulation often.

### Good Topics

- Professional requests
- Debate and opinion
- Cultural differences
- Workplace hierarchy
- Media and news discussion
- Complex relationships and emotions
