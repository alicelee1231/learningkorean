# Material Creation Workflow

## Core Idea

Use AI to speed up lesson creation, then make a final human correction so the Korean stays natural and level-appropriate.

## Workflow

1. Choose one lesson situation.
2. Decide the learner level and speaking goal.
3. Write the situation in the learner's native language or in English.
4. Ask AI to convert it into natural Korean.
5. Ask AI for:
   - a standard version
   - a natural spoken version
   - a more polite version
6. Select the best version and edit it manually.
7. Extract grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation points.
8. Create substitution drills and roleplay extensions.
9. Add personalized speaking questions.
10. Save the final lesson under the right level or topic.

## Quality Checklist

- Does this sound like something a Korean speaker would actually say?
- Is the level appropriate?
- Is the politeness level correct for the situation?
- Are there too many new words in one lesson?
- Is the grammar explanation short and useful?
- Is there enough speaking time?
- Is there at least one pronunciation focus?

## Avoid

- Overly translated sentences
- Too many grammar points in one class
- Vocabulary lists without context
- Long explanations that reduce speaking time
- Dialogues that sound scripted but not natural
